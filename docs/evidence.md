# Evidence and interpretation

## Scope

The reports combine previously recorded project results with the supplied files. Packaging verifies file structure, links and selected arithmetic. It does not refresh Power BI, execute SQL against a database, or independently audit every historical query result.

## Latest chatbot evidence

The v3 classification matches the intent-design document and the HTML analysis section. The supplied sample provides a weighted eligibility projection of 13.98%; it does not provide measured self-service success. The central scenario is 8.39% at an assumed 60% success rate. The handoff screenshot evidences one transfer with a human reply. The updated report uses these distinctions.

The supplied presentation retains its earlier 18% assumption-only scenario and earlier pending-evidence labels. The [presentation notes](../presentation/README.md) explain the change. Earlier material is not proof of current measured performance.

## Snapshot differences and limitations

- The later recorded status-conflict snapshot is 3,870 job cards / 3,750 tickets. The earlier 4,103 count is not treated as a measured improvement because the difference between snapshots is not documented.
- The original duplicate-removal counts and complete fresh-import execution record are not established by the reports. Script review is distinct from execution evidence.
- 3,998 complaints lack confirmed dates; lifetime counts include them while confirmed-month series exclude them.
- 4,211 resolved/closed complaints lack resolution dates. TAT and CSAT measures have eligibility conditions.
- Raw social timestamps have seven digits. The assumed reconstruction is illustrative and does not establish actual event timing.
- Job-card history is available for 2024 only.
- Cumulative recorded sales are a fleet proxy with zero opening balance, not a count of the entire active national fleet.
- Registration shares describe the six OEMs in the project dataset.
- Forecasts cover January-June 2025 as a historical exercise. Holdout results informed model selection and are not an independent final test.
- Sales correlations and the before/after test do not identify a causal effect of the regulatory notice or service intervention.
- The proposed centre allocation uses a benchmark of 1,000 recorded vehicles per centre. The calculated allocation sums to 100; the benchmark is not measured workshop capacity.

## Source roles

The course files in `data/` retain their original instructions and synthetic-data description. Those instructions are source material, not new completion tasks for the author. The source ledger cited by the historical reports is not supplied as a standalone file. No missing evidence has been fabricated.

The FAQ and fictional support records are demonstration material. They should not be read as verified current warranty, refund or repair policies. The chatbot does not establish access to live Ola systems.
