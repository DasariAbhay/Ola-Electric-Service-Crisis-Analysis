What changed after reviewing complaint examples

  • Added unavailable slots, missed pickups, missing updates and tickets closed without work to service-delay routing.
  • Prioritised explicit key-sharing problems over generic software-update mentions.
  • Retained BMS errors under Battery / range when the display merely reports the underlying problem.
  • Expanded historical safety screening to include sparking, severe handle vibration, unintended vehicle operation, sudden power loss while riding and motor overheating warnings.

The 30,873 safety matches are screening candidates. Keyword classification does not establish that every record is a confirmed emergency. Conversational safety handling requires a clearly reported hazard and must account for negation and context.

Illustrative estimated deflection: 8.39%

The reported stratified review covered 100 historical synthetic complaints across Battery / range, Software / display, Charger / charging and App / connectivity. These groups contain 62,129 tickets, representing 47.29% of the full corpus.

Weighted eligible-request share
13.98% of all historical tickets, based on the reported sample review and weighting by historical group size.
Central resolution assumption
60% of eligible requests successfully resolved through self-service. This is an explicit assumption, not an observed operational rate.
Illustrative estimated deflection
13.98% eligible share multiplied by 60% assumed resolution gives approximately 8.39%.
Sensitivity range
Assuming 40%–80% resolution produces approximately 5.59%–11.19% estimated deflection.

Safety cases and requests requiring operational or physical intervention are excluded. These are planning estimates, not measured avoided contacts, realised savings or population-level performance. The small sample, rule-based grouping and repetitive synthetic text limit generalisation.

Demonstrated capabilities and operational limits

  • Ticket lookup uses five fictional records and labels results as simulated. Unknown IDs must not produce invented statuses.
  • Service-centre lookup returns project listings matched by city. It does not establish the nearest location or live appointment availability.
  • Recorded capacity is measured in jobs per day. It is not the number of appointment slots currently available.
  • Booking guidance explains the supplied process; the demo does not reserve appointments or issue booking confirmations.
  • Human handoff reached Botpress Desk, an agent replied, and closing the conversation returned control to the chatbot, according to the completed walkthrough.
  • Human chat transfer does not confirm a callback, repair, refund, service appointment or operational ticket creation.
